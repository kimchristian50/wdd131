Family Photography

With 20+ years of professional photography experience, my goal in a custom family photography session is to make the whole experience enjoyable, relaxed, and easy.

I don't expect your children to behave any certain way - I want them to be themselves. I love children and have lots of ideas to make our time together an adventure instead of a chore. I want our experience to be so fun for all involved that your family will want to do it again for many years to come.

My custom sessions include:

  • A phone or in-person consultation to get to know you, figure out the best location for you, and help with what to wear.
  • Sessions can be in my home studio, or outside in the greater Albuquerque area.
  • We will spend one to two hours together with my camera. thing you will remember
  • An in-person ordering session where I can help you choose the best images and products for your home and your needs.

For this, my session fee is $150, paid in advance.
