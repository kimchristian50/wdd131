What are sessions like?

Where will we go?

When you choose the full session, we can go anywhere in the Albuquerque area, and often we can go to a couple of places if we plan our time right!

If you choose a half session we will pick just one location, but there are lots of great places in town that can give us great variety. When we plan your session I will have lots of ideas for you to think about.

I can also do studio portraits in my home studio if that's your preference.

What should I bring?

That depends on you! If you play sports or have hobbies that are important to you, I'd love to include that in your portraits. I've done senior portraits with soccer gear, javelins, baseball gear, basketballs, volleyballs, track shoes, bowling balls, hunting gear, and stacks of medals and trophies. I've done racing portraits in the pool and on the track and sometimes I go to meets or games. I've included violins, flutes, trombones, guitars, ukuleles, grand pianos, and once a tuba. Nursing students have brought their stethoscope; avid readers bring their favorite books. I've done sessions with seniors who love drama on a stage or in their costumes and sessions with art students and their paintings. Some seniors have brought a dog, cat, or horse. If it's an important part of your life, I'd love to include that in your senior session.

FAQs
  1. Do you retouch? Yes! I retouch every image that you purchase. Your skin will look perfect yet natural.
  2. Do you sell digital files? Yes. Included with your purchase are sharing-sized images - you'll receive a small digital version of every image that you purchase. If you're interested in purchasing full-size images that is also possible.
  3. How many outfits can I bring? Bring a lot of them. My sessions are limited by time, not by outfits or poses. You can change as many times as you like within the session time (2 - 3 hours for a full session, 1 hour for a half session).
  4. Can I bring a friend? Yes! Friends make everything more fun. I also love it when moms, dads, brothers, sisters, etc., come along. Bring someone fun who can make you laugh!.
  5. Can I bring my pet? Sure! Just please make sure I know you're planning on a pet ahead of time. If your pet is a horse, please let me come to you.
  6. Can I share my senior session with a friend? Yes! Having a friend along makes everything more fun. If you want to combine your senior session with a friend, I will discount each of your session fees by $50. You can get pictures together, and it also makes the viewing appointment even more fun.
